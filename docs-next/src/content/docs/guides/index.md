Guides explain the core concepts and workflows behind Training Gym. Use them
to understand how the pieces fit together; use the
[tutorials](/tutorials/) for complete runnable examples and the
[API reference](/reference/) for individual classes and fields.

## Tools

- [The observability dashboard](/guides/tools/observability-dashboard/) — track
  runs, inspect rollouts, and profile step timings.
- [Weights & Biases integration](/guides/tools/wandb-integration/) — stream
  training curves and connect Training Gym runs to W&B.
